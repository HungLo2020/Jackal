Player weapons: recovery evidence and native mapping

Source: fixed bank ED2C..F07F in original game data, historical reference commit
 e8fc3391d5b27fcd98b82fd27984141840449708.
Reproduce: python3 tools/reference/export_player_weapons.py --check
Captured scenarios: gun, grenade/rocket in all eight directions, both blast-wave axes.
All positions, frames, flips and active lifetimes are compared frame-by-frame in
 tests/player_weapons.rs. This includes initialization and removal states.

Gun ED2C: initialization frame stationary; timer16, speed6 upward. Fifteen travel
frames cover90 pixels. Timer expiry stops motion and shows frame17 for5ticks, then
removes the shot. Frame16 in flight. Direct enemy hits remove bullets (E3EA), while
terrain/fuse end shows impact (ED7A); callers must distinguish those outcomes.

Grenade EDE8: speed2 cardinal,1.5 each diagonal,40 travel ticks after initialization
(50 at final HQ). Flight frames7/8/7 and global-frame-based flips are recovered.
Pending impact state then central explosion frames25/26/27,8ticks each. Slot stays
reserved through the explosion. Total normal lifetime66 updates including init/remove.

Rocket EFBA: speed4 cardinal,2.75 diagonal (not3);22 travel ticks after initialization
(32 at final HQ). Frames13/14/15 with original eight-way flips; no white primitive.
After pending impact, same24tick central explosion. Total normal lifetime48 updates.

Upgrades EF42/F039: TwoWay creates one horizontal wave actor; FourWay creates an
additional vertical actor. Wave has initialization,40active updates and removal.
Positions alternate relative to origin: +4,-6,+8,-10,...,+80,-82. It does not sweep
all space between those points. Frames9 untiltimer<24,10 untiltimer<8,then11.
Vertical flip toggles on global tick multiples of4. Wave actor lifetime42 updates.
Normal secondary slot can be reused once central blast ends even if wave persists.

Collision E414..E4D2: unarmored enemies are killed directly by secondary hits;
armored HP receives5damage from the direct projectile in flight only. Armored
objects reject central explosion states and wave slots. Original interaction flag
0x10 lets a secondary pass through the target; exported secondary_passes_through
preserves this. Other direct collisions switch grenade/rocket to pending explosion.
Hitboxes are axis-aligned and secondary adds4pixels to original target half extents.
Wave collisions must use its current alternating position, never a swept line.

These isolated traces prove phase behavior on clear terrain and fixed camera.
They do not establish all integration timing under scrolling, actor slot pressure,
co-op shared slots, or every terrain transition; those need separate scene tests.
